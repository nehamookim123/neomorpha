Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty) in Ranchi

Eyelid surgery removes excess skin and fat from droopy upper lids or puffy lower lids.

It can reduce under-eye bags and the heaviness that makes eyes look tired, so the eyes appear more open after healing.

In some cases, where sagging upper lids block sight, it can also improve the field of vision.
