Hair Rejuvenation in Ranchi

Hair rejuvenation treatments aim to support thinning hair and improve scalp health.

The right approach depends on the cause of hair loss, so every plan starts with a consultation and assessment.

Consultation

Every treatment starts with a consultation with Dr. Neha Mookim to understand your concerns, examine, and discuss whether the procedure suits you, along with its risks, recovery and alternatives.
