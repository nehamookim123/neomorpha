Our doctors

Meet the doctors

Clinique Neo Morpha is led by two surgeons, each heading one wing of the clinic.

Consultant Plastic Surgeon · Head, Neo Sculpt

Dr. Neha Mookim

MBBS, MS (General Surgery), MCh (Plastic Surgery), MRCS (Glasgow)

Dr. Neha Mookim is a plastic surgeon whose work spans aesthetic surgery, burns, reconstruction and microsurgery.

Trained at leading institutes in Southern India, she moved to plastic surgery after a decade of general surgery practice. Her interests are burns and reconstructive surgery, with a strong focus on aesthetics.

Her aim is to build a dedicated centre for plastic, reconstructive and aesthetic surgery in Jharkhand.

I truly believe that everyone is beautiful in their own way. Sometimes, though, the way we look on the outside doesn't fully reflect how we feel on the inside. That's when a little change can make a big difference — not to change who you are, but to let your best self shine through.

For me, plastic surgery is about balance and confidence. It's about finding the right procedure, done in the right way, for the right person. My role is to listen, guide, and help you make choices that feel right for you.

Consultant Orthopaedic Surgeon · Head, Neo Sport

Dr. Anubhav Jain

MS (Orthopaedics), MRCS (Glasgow)

Dr. Anubhav Jain is an orthopaedic surgeon who leads Neo Sport, the clinic's orthopaedics and sports medicine wing.

Neo Sport focuses on helping patients recover movement, strength and everyday function after injury or joint problems.
