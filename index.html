Treatments

Common treatments at Neo Sculpt

Rhinoplasty (Nose Reshaping)

Rhinoplasty, or nose reshaping, changes the size or shape of the nose to better match the rest of the face.

Learn more →

Facelift

A facelift is a surgery that lifts and tightens sagging skin of the face and neck.

Learn more →

Eyelid Surgery (Blepharoplasty)

Eyelid surgery removes excess skin and fat from droopy upper lids or puffy lower lids.

Learn more →

Botox, Fillers & Laser Treatments

Injectables such as Botox and dermal fillers are minimally invasive treatments used to smooth wrinkles, restore lost volume and enhance facial features.

Learn more →

Liposuction

Liposuction removes stubborn fat that does not go away with diet or exercise. It can shape areas such as the tummy, thighs, hips, arms, chin and back.

Learn more →

Tummy Tuck (Abdominoplasty)

A tummy tuck removes extra skin and fat from the abdomen and tightens weak or stretched abdominal muscles.

Learn more →

Gynaecomastia (Male Breast Reduction)

Gynaecomastia is enlargement of the male breast due to extra fat or glandular tissue. It can affect confidence and cause discomfort.

Learn more →

Burn Surgery & Burn Scars

Burn care covers both fresh burns and long-standing burn scars.

Meet the surgeon

Dr. Neha Mookim

MBBS, MS (General Surgery), MCh (Plastic Surgery), MRCS (Glasgow)

Dr. Neha Mookim is a plastic surgeon whose work spans aesthetic surgery, burns, reconstruction and microsurgery. Trained at leading institutes in Southern India, she moved to plastic surgery after a decade of general surgery practice. Her interests are burns and reconstructive surgery, with a strong focus on aesthetics.

For me, plastic surgery is about balance and confidence — the right procedure, done the right way, for the right person.
